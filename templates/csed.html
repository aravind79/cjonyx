Why CSED Exists

Why was Cjonyx Center for Social Enterprise Development created?

Cjonyx Social Enterprise Development Center is a self-sustaining hybrid benefit corporation providing blended values through both social and profit benefits. The center is committed to community research and needs analysis, while training people who are about to retire and retirees to develop and express a desire for social entrepreneurship. The goal is to strategically steer retirees toward community support based on identified needs.

Research

We study community realities so interventions respond to actual local needs.

Transition Support

We help pre-retirees and retirees redirect experience into social enterprise impact.

Blended Value

CSED is built around programs that combine social good with sustainable enterprise outcomes.
